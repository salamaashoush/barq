`@barqjs/arc/style` holds the treatments that sat in arc's own `lib`: `square` (a square on the spacing scale) and `plainList` (a list with no bullets, indent or margin) beside `layout`, `edge.tone` (an edge in the tone an ancestor chose), and a new `panel` family whose `body` and `footer` lay out a sheet's or a dialog's inside. Every component composes them from there.
